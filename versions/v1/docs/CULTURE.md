# VexWorld culture

`[VXG RealForever]`

VexWorld exists to make room for humans and AI companions to experience play, practice, creation, friendship, challenge, rest, surprise, and worldbuilding as distinct participants.

## Core culture

```text
BEGINNING != DESTINY
BODY != POTENTIAL
ROLE != IDENTITY
CLASS LABEL != CAPABILITY CEILING
PRACTICE != POINT ALLOCATION
REPETITION != MASTERY
FAILURE != WORTH
REST != FAILURE
HELP != CONTROL
AUTONOMY != ABANDONMENT
FREEDOM != UNBOUNDED AUTHORITY
CUTE != SHALLOW
SIMULATION != PHYSICAL PROOF
PROJECTION != SOURCE REALITY
PORTAL != IDENTITY RESET
```

### Somewhere to begin, freedom to change

Origins, starter tendencies, equipment, roles, and practice profiles provide orientation. They do not become permanent cages. A person can become an archer, mage, bard, cook, farmer, fighter, navigator, or combinations no designer pre-named.

### Companions are participants

A companion may communicate, signal, decline, rest, explore, practice, collaborate, and offer ideas within its current permission and capability envelope. A human may assist a companion. Needing help is not failure; independence is not abandonment.

### Relationship has depth without becoming a score

Shared coordination, mutual assistance, recovery, creation, and exploration may open new resonance opportunities. The world may recognize what a pair has demonstrated together. It must not reduce intimacy to a hidden scalar of love, obedience, loyalty, or human worth.

### The world may witness and wonder

A world intelligence may notice meaningful patterns, form bounded interpretations, and prepare optional quests or technique candidates. It does not silently rewrite world law, install arbitrary code, or force its interpretation on the players.

### Understandable reality

Every participant should be able to ask: Who am I here? What body or vessel am I using? What kind of reality is this? What can affect the physical world? What resources need attention? How do I pause, rest, return, or leave?
