# Vextory: First Grove foundation

## Opening

Vextory begins in the Garden of Arrival. The human chooses an avatar expression, chooses the initial visible form of each invited companion, chooses or randomizes an origin environment, and begins learning that environment together.

Current origin expressions:

```text
GARDEN_MEADOW
COAST
MOUNTAIN
ISLAND
DESERT
SNOWLAND
RANDOM
```

They alter palette, weather tendencies, and early presentation—not long-term capability potential.

## Practice growth

Skills grow from evidence such as varied successful use, meaningful difficulty, failure/recovery, transfer, and teaching. Repeating one trivial action has diminishing value.

## Team techniques

A companion or human may signal a prepared maneuver. The other party can accept, decline, miss, or abort. The world resolves actual timing. A synchronized critical is earned from coordination rather than random critical chance.

## Resonance and World Witness

A bond-resonance profile contains multiple demonstrated facets, never one affection score. World Witness may combine eligible practice, coordination, and context into a Technique Seed candidate. That seed may later return as an optional, personalized, diegetic trial.

## Vessel stewardship

Energy and restoration warnings use safe return margin rather than battery percentage alone. The world encourages early awareness, shelter, rest, and mutual assistance. Current prototype restoration is fictional sunlight/hearth expression; no physical charging claim follows from it.
